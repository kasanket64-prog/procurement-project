Is this a production AI platform?

It is a local prototype. Real-world prediction reliability and buyer acceptance are still pending.

Does the platform purchase automatically?

No. It never sends an order, payment or supplier commitment.

What data is shown in the showcase?

Artificial examples demonstrate the workflow. They are not real supplier performance or live workspace results.

Where do imported reports go?

They stay in the current page. No automatic upload or persistence occurs.

Can I use genuine buyer data?

Only with valid permission, protected storage and reviewed mappings. Prototype checks do not establish consent.
