# Public procurement sample

Retrieved 15 November 2026 from official APIs. `manifest.json` preserves request URLs, POST bodies, retrieval timestamps and SHA-256 hashes of the original responses. `records.json` is a display projection, with official record links; original responses remain unchanged.

- UK Contracts Finder: one page of 100 award releases published in 2025. Reuse: Open Government Licence v3.0, as stated in the original response. Award totals are not unit prices.
- USAspending: 100 contract-award records returned by a 2025 activity filter, sorted by award amount descending. Contracts may have started earlier. Published totals are not 2025-only spending or unit prices. Source documentation: https://api.usaspending.gov/docs/endpoints
- EU TED: 100 procurement notices published in 2025, with English titles where provided. Notice types are mixed; these are not all awards or supplier leads. Source documentation: https://docs.ted.europa.eu/api/latest/search.html

These are bounded, nonrepresentative samples, not global supplier coverage. Countries of publication do not establish supplier location or delivery destinations. No current quotation, stock, brand authorization, delivery outcome, or supplier reliability is inferred. Public records do not enter demo scoring, delivery-risk training, or genuine-quotation comparison. Source-specific reuse conditions still apply; no new blanket licence is asserted by this package.
